Selling a place, not a plan

The winning concept in testing led on the theme and the scale — live, work, relax within one development — rather than on configuration and price.

At township scale the buyer is choosing an environment they will live inside for a decade. Leading with square footage answers a question they have not reached yet.

The East Bangalore constraint

Whitefield is the most commute-decided catchment in the city. A project twenty minutes further out is in a different market, not a slightly worse one, so the catchment is drawn tightly and geography does real work in the qualification standard.

It is also where search budgets die: rental, resale and PG queries surround every property term here, and the negative keyword list has to be built before launch rather than after the first invoice.
