Pricing

Real estate marketing pricing

Updated 13 October 2026

Meta/Google ad campaign management is priced by campaign or project count. Your written estimate separates agency fees, media spend and any additional services.

Tell us your business type, number of projects and service needs. We will confirm the monthly scope and estimate before work starts. QDnCo no longer offers the historical per-qualified-lead pricing described in older versions of this page.

Meta/Google ad campaigns

For developers, mandate firms and channel partners. The campaign scope includes:

  • Market research and positioning
  • Campaign strategy, setup and launch
  • Unlimited ad creatives as required by the agreed scope, without per-creative charges
  • Meta asset creation as required, without an additional asset-setup charge
  • Campaign maintenance and weekly reporting
  • A dedicated relationship manager

Campaign count determines the estimate

Share how many projects or campaigns need management. Fees vary by business type and campaign count. Confirm the applicable tier, duration and exclusions in a dated written quote. Lead qualification is a performance measure, not a separate billable unit or a replacement guarantee.

Research and setup

Market research and strategy form part of the agreed campaign scope. Any separate consulting work requires its own written scope and estimate.

Other services

Real Estate SEO & AI Search Optimization, brand presence and PR, CRM Setup & Automation, Landing Pages, and AI Lead Follow-Up & WhatsApp Automation receive custom quotes. Campaign-management pricing does not automatically apply to these services.

Media and production are separate

Pay Meta and Google directly from your own ad account. Agree the media budget before launch. Shoots, drone footage and specialist production require a separate scope if needed. Confirm applicable taxes and exclusions in writing.

Dubai pricing

Realtor / channel partner campaign management starts at AED 4,650 per project per month. Project-count tiers apply; developers and mandate firms share a separate schedule. Discuss your Dubai scope.

Pricing questions

Do you bill per qualified lead?

No. Current Meta/Google ad campaign fees are based on campaign or project count and scope. Historical per-lead offers have been retired.

Is media included?

No. Agency fees and platform media spend are separate.

Are results guaranteed?

No. We agree service deliverables and reporting. Lead quality and bookings also depend on the project, pricing, market and sales follow-up.

How do I get an estimate?

Share your business type, service and campaign count. We will confirm the scope and pricing with you.
