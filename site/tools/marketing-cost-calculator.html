How your fee is worked out

QDnCo manages Meta and Google ad campaigns for real estate developers, mandate firms and channel partners. The monthly fee depends on three things, and the calculator shows it once you share your name and number:

  • Who you are. Developers and mandate firms share one rate card; channel partners and realtors have their own.
  • How many campaigns or projects you run. The fee per campaign falls as the count rises. In Dubai, pricing is per project in AED, with one campaign per project.
  • Which service you need. Meta/Google campaigns have a fixed calculator price. SEO and AI search, landing pages, CRM setup and WhatsApp automation are scoped and quoted for you.

Media spend is separate: you pay Meta and Google directly from your own ad account, and it is never billed through us. Engagements run for three months, and the final scope is confirmed in writing after a project review. Studio production (shoots, drone, UGC, motion graphics) is quoted separately.
