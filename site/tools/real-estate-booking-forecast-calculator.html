How this calculator works

The source is QDnCo's house planning model. It is built down from a delivered ₹40,000 test on a premium Bengaluru residential project: 150+ leads at ₹266 per lead, 51% scored sales-ready by the developer's own team (owner-reported). At ₹3.08 lakh a month it reproduces the forecast used in a live 2026 proposal. These source results are not independently reconciled to the platform export and CRM cohort.

Questions about the forecast

How many site visits should a ₹3 lakh monthly real estate ad budget produce?

On QDnCo's planning model, ₹3 lakh a month split evenly between Meta and Google forecasts about 100 site visits in Month 3 and about 165 across the first three months, assuming new leads are called within five minutes. Month 1 is lower because campaigns run only about 16 days after setup.

What is a good cost per lead for real estate in Bengaluru?

QDnCo plans Month-3 cost per lead at ₹350–₹550 blended across Meta and Google for mid-ticket residential projects, after a learning-phase Month 1 of ₹750–₹950. Cost per lead means little unless it is paired with a qualification rate and a site-visit rate.

Why is Month 1 cost per lead higher?

The first two weeks go on research, creative and approvals, so Month 1 has about 16 live days, and the platforms are still learning which leads qualify. QDnCo's model plans Month 1 at ₹750–₹950 per lead and Month 3 at ₹350–₹550.

Meta or Google Ads for real estate: which gives more bookings?

Meta gives more leads per rupee and Google gives warmer leads. QDnCo's planning model prices Google leads about 40% higher than Meta leads but qualifies them about 12 points higher. Most developers get the most site visits from a mix, using Meta to create demand and Google to capture it.

What does "qualified lead" mean in this calculator?

A lead is counted as qualified only when three facts match: the buyer wants the project's location, the unit configuration they want is available, and their stated budget covers the all-in price. The developer's sales team scores it, not the agency.

How much does lead follow-up speed affect site visits?

In QDnCo's model, calling the next day instead of within five minutes cuts expected site visits by about 45%. It is the largest lever a developer controls without spending more on ads.
