Accessibility Statement

Last updated 2026-09-19 · Applies to https://qdnco.com

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. This statement records where it stands, honestly.

1. What has been done

  • Contrast — text over the background video is measured against the rendered pixels, not estimated, and holds at or above the AA threshold.
  • Touch targets — interactive controls are at least 44×44 CSS pixels.
  • Responsive — tested from 272px to 4K with no horizontal scrolling and no clipped text.
  • Reduced motion — the marquees, the ad reel and the decorative animations stop for anyone whose system requests reduced motion.
  • Semantics — one h1, an unbroken heading order, real landmarks, alt text on meaningful images and empty alt on decorative ones.
  • Both themes — a light and a dark version, following the system setting by default.

2. Known limitations

  • Several sections reveal content as you scroll. The content is present without scrolling for assistive technology, but the visual sequence assumes a scrolling device.
  • The ad creative shown in the reel is decorative and marked as such; the text inside those images is not available to a screen reader. The claims in them are described in the surrounding copy.
  • The background video is decorative and has no captions, because it carries no information.

3. Reporting a barrier

If something on this site is unusable for you, tell us and we will fix it. Email qdnco01@gmail.com or call +91 88611 79992. Please say which page and what happened. We reply within 5 working days.

If you need any information from this site in another format — plain text, large print, or read to you over the phone — ask, and we will provide it.
