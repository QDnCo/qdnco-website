The India → Dubai Real Estate Buyer Acquisition Report 2026

Published 2026-09-29 · snapshots 26–29 September 2026

Indian nationals were the largest foreign buyer group in Dubai property in 2025. This report looks at the other side of that demand: what Dubai developers and brokers actually show people in India, what Indians search before they enquire, and where the interest is concentrated. It combines 521 Dubai property ads shown in India, 984 ads shown inside the UAE, 1,014 Google search suggestions and Google Trends. Every record is downloadable.

No spend, lead or sales data is included.

4. The offers being used

  • The payment plan is the offer. 73% of India-facing ads lead with one: small booking amounts, monthly instalments and post-handover splits.
  • Rupee pricing. 31% quote a rupee price or a monthly amount in rupees, against 18% in AED.
  • Events. 26% promote a property show, roadshow or seminar, usually in a named Indian city.
  • Residency. 21% mention the Golden Visa; the real estate route requires property worth at least AED 2 million, about ₹5.2 crore at ₹26.17 (UAE Ministry of Economy — Golden Visa for real estate investors).
  • Tax framing. 6.5% use "tax-free" language, while 1.5% say anything about loans, remittance or Indian tax, the questions Indians actually search.

6. The acquisition funnel, India → Dubai

  1. Ad shown in India, carrying the DLD permit number and QR code (DLD — advertising governance, 24 April 2025).
  2. Landing page answering the seven questions above, with prices in AED and rupees.
  3. Qualification: budget and currency, funding route (savings, loan, remittance), timeline, residency status, visit plan.
  4. WhatsApp reply within minutes, in Indian Standard Time.
  5. Sales conversation with the licensed Dubai broker or developer.
  6. Visit: a Dubai trip, a virtual viewing, or a property show in an Indian city.
  7. Booking, with instalments planned against the remittance limit.

Questions about this report

How many Dubai property ads are shown to people in India?

Our 29 September 2026 snapshot of Meta's Ad Library with the country set to India found 521 active Dubai property ads from 216 advertisers across 14 search terms. Meta's own result counts for broader terms ran to tens of thousands, so this is a keyword-selected sample, not a census.

How do Dubai property ads aimed at India differ from ads shown in the UAE?

They lean far harder on the payment plan (73% against 41% of UAE ads), price in rupees (31%) more often than in AED (18%), and a quarter promote an expo or event in an Indian city. Very few name NRIs (0.6%) and none of the snapshot used Hindi script.

Which Indian states search most for Dubai property?

In Google Trends over the past 12 months, relative interest in 'property in Dubai' was highest in Delhi, Haryana, Goa, Maharashtra, Karnataka. Trends shows relative interest, not search volumes.

What do Indians search about Dubai property?

Across 1,014 unique Google suggestions collected from India, the largest themes were price and budget (often in rupees), rules and tax for Indians, specific Dubai areas, renting out and yield, and residency or Golden Visa.

Does QDnCo publish Dubai cost-per-lead benchmarks?

Not yet. We will publish India→Dubai campaign benchmarks only when we have first-party campaign data, labelled as observed, with the date window and sample.
