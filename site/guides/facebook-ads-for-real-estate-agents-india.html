Facebook and Instagram ads for real estate agents in India: setup, budget and lead quality

Last updated 2026-09-26 · 9 min read

Meta ads can fill a broker's pipeline in a week, or fill it with people who never answer. The difference is not the creative. It is the account setup, the form, and how fast someone calls.

The short answer

Run Meta ads from your own business account, one campaign per project, with the price range and RERA number on the creative, an instant form that asks budget and timeline, and a phone call plus WhatsApp reply within minutes. Set the test budget from the number of leads you need to judge the campaign, and scale only what your CRM shows qualifying.

Account setup that you own

  1. Business portfolio in your brokerage's name. Create the ad account, Facebook page and Instagram account under your own business, not a freelancer's personal profile. If an agency runs it, give it partner access.
  2. Pixel and Conversions API. Install the Meta pixel on your landing pages and connect your CRM so Meta learns which leads became site visits, not just which filled a form.
  3. Verify the domain and a payment method in your name, so the account and its history cannot be lost with a vendor.

Campaign structure for a broker

  • One campaign per project, so cost and quality are readable per mandate.
  • Location by catchment, not by city. A Hebbal project draws buyers from specific localities and workplaces; target those and exclude areas that never book.
  • Keep partner recruitment separate from buyer ads. Broker-network campaigns and end-buyer campaigns want different creative and cannot share a cost-per-lead target.
  • Retarget people who watched your walkthrough video or opened the form without submitting.

A form that filters

Instant forms convert because they are easy, which is also why they attract low intent. Add two or three questions: budget range, purchase timeline and preferred locality. Choose Meta's higher-intent form type, which adds a review screen before submission. Put the price range on the ad itself so buyers outside the budget filter themselves out.

Creative that sells a site visit

  • Lead with the reason to visit: price, payment plan, possession date or location, one per ad.
  • Show the project's RERA registration number, and follow state rules such as MahaRERA's QR code requirement for Maharashtra projects (The Realty Today — MahaRERA makes QR code and registration number mandatory in ads).
  • Use real walkthrough video where the developer allows it; it filters out brochure collectors.
  • Test one variable at a time, such as price-led against location-led for the same project.

Setting the test budget

Work backwards. Decide how many leads you need to judge a campaign, say about 60, and multiply by the cost per lead you expect. At ₹600 per lead that is about ₹36,000 of media for the test. Agree that cap before launch, and judge the result on qualified leads and site visits, not on the cost per lead alone. Our own recent figures, published on our homepage: cost per form lead of ₹395–₹876 across four Bengaluru and Mysore campaigns between 24 August and 22 September 2026. These are form leads, not qualified leads or sales, and each covers one campaign only.

Follow-up decides the result

Research on online leads found that companies which tried to contact a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as those that waited even an hour longer (Harvard Business Review — The Short Life of Online Sales Leads, March 2011). Send an automatic WhatsApp message the moment the form arrives and call within minutes. See WhatsApp follow-up for real estate leads.

Rules to check before you run

  • Your state RERA agent registration, and that the project is registered (MahaRERA — guidance for real estate agents).
  • Your mandate with the developer allows you to advertise the project, and follows its brand rules.
  • Whether Meta asks you to declare a special ad category for the campaign; if it does, targeting narrows.

Questions channel partners ask

How much should a real estate agent spend on Facebook ads in India?

Set the test budget from the cost per lead you can afford and the number of leads you need to judge a campaign. For example, if you need about 60 leads to read results and expect ₹600 per lead, the test costs about ₹36,000 in media. Agree the cap before launch and scale only if leads qualify.

Should I use Facebook instant forms or a landing page?

Instant forms produce more leads at a lower cost per lead; landing pages usually produce fewer, better ones. If you use instant forms, add a question on budget and one on timeline, and use Meta's higher-intent form type that adds a review step. Compare the two on cost per qualified lead.

Can I run ads for a project I am selling as a channel partner?

Yes, if you are registered as an agent with the state RERA authority where required, the project is registered, and your mandate with the developer allows you to advertise. Show the project's RERA number and follow any developer brand guidelines in your agreement.

Why are my Facebook real estate leads fake or not answering?

Usually the form is too easy. Pre-filled instant forms let people submit with one tap. Add qualifying questions, use the higher-intent form type, call within minutes, and send an automatic WhatsApp reply. Check that the ad states a price or price range so people outside the budget do not enquire.

Do I need to choose a special ad category for housing in India?

Meta requires a special ad category for housing ads in certain countries, which narrows targeting. Check the prompt in Ads Manager when you create a campaign targeting India; if Meta asks for it, targeting options will be limited accordingly.
