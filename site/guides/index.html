Last updated 2026-09-29 · India and Dubai

Working guides from QDnCo for real estate developers, brokerages and channel partners in India and Dubai. Everything comes out of running paid acquisition for residential launches and from our own published research. Where a number appears it is labelled: observed, client-reported, a model or a market estimate.

Most agency content in this category is written to rank, not to be used. These are written the other way round: each one answers a question a developer or channel partner has actually asked on a call, in the order the question is usually asked in. If a guide tells you not to spend money, that is the guide working.
