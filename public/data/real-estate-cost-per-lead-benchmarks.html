The first example gives about ₹522 per sales-ready lead by arithmetic. It does not establish the cost of a booking or collected revenue.

Compare useful leads on consistent definitions

Illustrative comparison: ₹200 CPL at a 15% useful-lead rate gives about ₹1,333 per useful lead. ₹600 CPL at 60% gives ₹1,000. The lower raw CPL has the higher useful-lead cost in this example.

Define the qualification criteria, duplicate window, reporting period and attribution method before comparing campaigns. Report the raw count and qualified count alongside percentages so small or incomplete cohorts are visible.

Test the drivers instead of assuming them

  • Offer and catchment. Ticket size, location and buyer readiness can change both acquisition cost and conversion.
  • Targeting. Broadening an audience may change volume and quality; the direction and size need to be measured.
  • Optimisation and feedback. CRM qualification signals can support a downstream test when the integration and data quality are reliable. They do not guarantee an improvement.
  • Follow-up. Contact speed, sales coverage and qualification consistency affect how many form submissions become useful conversations.

Build a booking-cost scenario

For an illustration only, assume a 1% raw-lead-to-booking rate and ₹400 CPL. Ten bookings would require 1,000 leads and ₹4 lakh in media before agency fees and applicable taxes. At 0.5% conversion the same target requires twice the media; at 2% it requires half.

Use completed sales cohorts to replace these assumptions. A recent lead cohort that has not finished its sales cycle cannot yet establish booking conversion.

Questions about this research

What is a good real estate CPL in Bengaluru?

There is no universal threshold established by these examples. Evaluate useful-lead and booking costs against your project economics; the ₹350–500 range here is a planning assumption, not a surveyed benchmark.

Is the ₹266 CPL independently verified?

No. It is a historical owner-reported example, paired with a reported 51% sales-ready rate. Dated platform and CRM reconciliation remains pending.

Are leads always cheaper in Tier-2 Karnataka?

This material does not establish that conclusion. Compare matching offers and complete cohorts in the relevant markets before assuming a geographic discount.
